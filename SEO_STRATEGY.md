## Phase 3: Off-Page SEO & Authority Building (User Action Required)

SEO is 50% content and 50% authority (backlinks).

1.  **Directory Listings**:
    *   Register your agency on **Clutch.co**, **GoodFirms**, and **UpCity**.
    *   Use the description: "Epsilon Technology provides affordable custom application development and website development services..."

2.  **Social Proof**:
    *   Ask your past clients to review you on Google My Business and Clutch.
    *   Reviews containing keywords like "Great mobile app developers" help your ranking.

3.  **Backlinks**:
    *   Write guest posts for tech blogs (Medium, Dev.to, or industry sites) and link back to your service pages.
